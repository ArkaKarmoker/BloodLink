Community Life-Saving Mission

About BloodLink

BloodLink is a dedicated humanitarian platform built to connect voluntary blood donors with patients, families, and healthcare providers in real time.

Our Mission

To eliminate deaths caused by delay in finding compatible blood donors. By removing middlemen and enabling direct communication between patients and donors, we ensure life-saving blood reaches those in critical condition swiftly.

Donor Safety & Ethics

Blood donation is 100% voluntary, altruistic, and safe. We promote the standard 90-day cooldown interval, encouraging healthy regular donations while safeguarding donor health at every step.

Who Can Donate Blood?

Basic health qualifications recommended by healthcare authorities:

Age & Weight
Between 18 and 60 years old, weighing at least 45–50 kg.
Donation Interval
At least 90 days (3 months) since your last whole blood donation.
Hemoglobin & Health
Normal blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin level (>= 12.5 g/dL).
No Chronic Illness
Free of active viral infections, hepatitis B/C, HIV, or heart ailments.
